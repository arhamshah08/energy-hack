- **ETSI MEC technical group**: Defines multi-access edge computing as cloud capabilities and an IT service environment at the network edge. (access: verified; tracks: 1,4; id: etsi-mec-overview) https://www.etsi.org/technical-groups/mec/
- **FERC Order No. 2222**: Requires RTOs/ISOs to let DER aggregations into energy, capacity and ancillary markets; minimum aggregation size no more than 100 kW. Does not apply to ERCOT. Implementation dates on the page are as of Aug 2024. (access: verified; tracks: 4; id: ferc-2222) https://www.ferc.gov/ferc-order-no-2222-explainer-facilitating-participation-electricity-markets-distributed-energy
- **FERC Order No. 2222 fact sheet**: Minimum aggregation size in RTO/ISO markets may be no greater than 100 kW. (access: verified; tracks: 3,4; id: ferc-2222-fact-sheet) https://www.ferc.gov/media/ferc-order-no-2222-fact-sheet
- **GEISA (Grid Edge Interoperability & Security Alliance)** by LF Energy community: Secure, interoperable ecosystem for deploying and operating grid edge computing and applications. LF Energy stage: Sandbox. Code: github.com/geisa. (access: verified; tracks: 4; id: lfe-geisa) https://lfenergy.org/projects/geisa/
- **IEEE 1547-2018**: Interconnection and interoperability standard for DERs connected to distribution grids. (access: verified; tracks: 2,4; id: ieee-1547) https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1547/5915/
- **IEEE 2030.5-2023 Smart Energy Profile**: Application protocol for utility communication with DERs; required for inverter communication under California Rule 21. (access: verified; tracks: 4; id: ieee-2030-5) https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/2030.5/11216/
- **ISO 15118-20: vehicle to grid communication interface**: Standard for communication between vehicle and charger, adding bidirectional power transfer to plug and charge and scheduling. Paid standard. (access: verified; tracks: 3,4; id: iso-15118-20) https://www.iso.org/standard/77845.html
- **MISO, Large Load Additions framework**: Requests seek connection in 18 to 36 months. Goal of approval within 120 days. Mechanisms include non-firm service, firm-service step-up, curtailment, telemetry, ride-through. (access: verified; tracks: 2,3,4; id: miso-large-load-integration) https://www.misoenergy.org/planning/large-loads---container-page/large-load-additions/
- **MISO, large load telemetry and PMU requirements**: Draft: real-time large load telemetry over ICCP and PMU measurements at the point of interconnection. (access: verified; tracks: 3,4; id: miso-telemetry-pmu) https://www.misoenergy.org/engage/MISO-Dashboard/operational-reliability-requirement---large-load-telemetry-and-phasor-measurement-unit-pmu-requirements/
- **MISO, LMR and demand response enrollment FAQ**: Minimums: LMR 0.1 MW, DRR 1 MW, EDR 0.1 MW. 2 to 6 hour notice, events up to 4 hours, up to 16 dispatches a year. Enrollment tool opens 1 Dec 2026; no API. (access: verified; tracks: 3,4; id: miso-lmr-faq) https://help.misoenergy.org/knowledgebase/article/KA-01560/en-us
- **OCPP: Open Charge Point Protocol (Open Charge Alliance)**: Open protocol between EV chargers and charging management systems, including smart charging profiles that cap or schedule charging power. - **OpenADR 3.0**: REST/OpenAPI standard for DR, price and DER signals; public YAML spec supplements 2.0a/b. Reference implementation is members-only; certification is separate from implementing the API. (access: verified; tracks: 3,4; id: openadr) https://www.openadr.org/openadr-3-0
- **OpenEAC Alliance M&V methodologies** by OpenEAC Alliance (repo maintained by WattCarbon): Open, peer-reviewed measurement and verification (M&V) methodologies for issuing Energy Attribute Certificates (EACs) per watt-hour or gram CO2e from battery storage, electrification, demand response and energy efficiency projects. Methods and statuses at methods.openeac.org; drafts go through a 30-day public comment period. (access: open; tracks: 3,4; id: open-eac-alliance) https://github.com/wattcarbon/open-eac-alliance
