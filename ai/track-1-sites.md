## Resources

### Data and datasets (36)

- **ACTIVSg10k: 10,000-bus synthetic grid, western US**: Synthetic grid on the US WECC footprint, 16 areas. Package includes a MATPOWER case, substation latitude/longitude in the PowerWorld .aux file, and an N-1 branch contingency list. No hourly load time series. Also bundled with MATPOWER as case_ACTIVSg10k. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: tamu-activsg10k) https://electricgrids.engr.tamu.edu/electric-grid-test-cases/activsg10k/
- **AFDC alternative fuel station locator (US DOE)**: US public EV charging stations with location, network, connector types and power level. API through the NREL developer network (free key). (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: afdc-stations) https://afdc.energy.gov/stations
- **Census TIGER/Line shapefiles (2026)**: National boundaries, roads, rails, hydrography; 2026 release 14 Oct 2026. Not parcels or zoning. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: census-tiger) https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html
- **CPUC High Fire Threat District map**: California fire threat tiers (Tier 2 elevated, Tier 3 extreme) used for utility fire safety rules, published as a map and ArcGIS feature service. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: cpuc-fire-threat-map) https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/wildfires/fire-threat-maps-and-fire-safety-rulemaking
- **EAGLE-I recorded electricity outages 2014 to 2022 (ORNL)**: Customers without power by US county at 15-minute intervals, 2014 to 2022, from DOE's EAGLE-I system. Live EAGLE-I portal: eagle-i.doe.gov. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: eagle-i-outages) https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24237376
- **EIA Energy Atlas, Electric Power Transmission Lines**: Transmission line GIS layer. Research could not retrieve metadata; an older EIA layer is archived. HIFLD legacy portal returned not-found. Check before relying on it. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: eia-transmission-lines) https://atlas.eia.gov/datasets/geoplatform::electric-power-transmission-lines
- **EIA state electricity data**: State-level capacity, generation, sales and related historical series. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: eia-state-data) https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/state/index.php
- **EIA-860 Annual Electric Generator Report**: Generator-level data for US plants of 1 MW and above: location, capacity, technology, status, planned additions and retirements. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: eia-860) https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/
- **EIA-860M monthly generator inventory (API)**: Monthly generator-level operating status and capacity via EIA API v2. (access: api-key; tracks: 1,2; id: eia-860m) https://www.eia.gov/opendata/browser/electricity/operating-generator-capacity/
- **EPA eGRID**: Emissions and generation mix for US power plants and grid subregions. Annual average emission rates. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: epa-egrid) https://www.epa.gov/egrid
- **Epoch AI data on AI data centers**: Tracked large AI data center projects with estimated power, compute and timelines. Focused on large sites, not edge. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: epoch-data-centers) https://epoch.ai/data/data-centers
- **ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus)**: Global hourly weather reanalysis from 1940. Common input for load, wind and solar forecasting. (access: registration; tracks: 1; id: era5) https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/
- **ESA WorldCover**: Global 10 m land cover maps for 2020 and 2021 from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: esa-worldcover) https://esa-worldcover.org/en
- **FCC National Broadband Map**: Provider-reported fixed and mobile broadband availability by location (CSV and GIS). Not fiber routes, capacity or latency. Download page blocks scripts. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: fcc-broadband-map) https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/
- **FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer**: Effective flood maps covering 90%+ of US population, via viewer, downloads and GIS services. Unmapped does not mean no flood risk. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: fema-nfhl) https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/national-flood-hazard-layer
- **FEMA National Risk Index (OpenFEMA)**: v1.20 (Dec 2025): risk for 18 hazards by state, county, tract. Tables, shapefiles, geodatabases, 39 to 411 MB. Old hazards.fema.gov/nri URL redirects. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: fema-nri) https://www.fema.gov/about/openfema/data-sets/national-risk-index-data
- **Grid Status (gridstatus.io): live and historical data for all US ISOs** by Grid Status: Web dashboards and API with live and historical prices (LMP), demand, forecasts and fuel mix across US ISOs: CAISO, ERCOT, PJM, MISO, SPP, NYISO, ISO-NE. Browse in the browser; an account is needed for the API and downloads. Python library: gridstatus. (access: mixed; tracks: 1,2,3,4; id: gridstatus-io) https://www.gridstatus.io/
- **Microsoft US Building Footprints**: Building footprint polygons for the US derived from imagery, by state, GeoJSON. ODbL license. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: microsoft-us-building-footprints) https://github.com/microsoft/USBuildingFootprints
- **MRLC Annual National Land Cover Database**: Annual US land cover, change and imperviousness 1985 to 2025 (Collection 1.2, June 2026). Downloads and WMS. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: mrlc-nlcd) https://www.mrlc.gov/
- **National Solar Radiation Database (NSRDB)**: Gridded hourly and sub-hourly solar irradiance and weather. Used to model on-site solar output at a location. (access: api-key; tracks: 1,3; id: nsrdb) https://nsrdb.nlr.gov/
- **National Zoning Atlas**: Standardized zoning for 11,000+ jurisdictions, 200+ characteristics per district. Data center is not a normalized permitted-use field. (access: mixed; tracks: 1; id: national-zoning-atlas) https://www.zoningatlas.org/
- **NODE Collective** by Building Decarbonization Coalition, Rewiring America, Eli Technologies: National open database of US electrification incentives and rebates. LF Energy stage: Sandbox. Code: github.com/NODE-Collective. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: lfe-node-collective) https://lfenergy.org/projects/node-collective/
- **Open Infrastructure Map**: Map of OpenStreetMap power lines, substations, power plants and telecom infrastructure. Underlying data via OSM / Overpass. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: openinframap) https://openinframap.org/
- **Open Sustainable Technology directory**: Directory and analysis of open-source projects in climate, sustainable energy and natural resources. Use it to find more tools. CC BY 4.0. (access: open; tracks: 1,2,3,4; id: open-sustainable-technology) https://github.com/protontypes/open-sustainable-technology
- **OpenEI Utility Rate Database**: US retail electricity tariffs including energy charges, demand charges and time-of-use periods. API available. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: openei-urdb) https://openei.org/wiki/Utility_Rate_Database
- **PeeringDB**: Database of colocation facilities, exchanges and networks with coordinates and a public API (peeringdb.com/apidocs). Facility records include diverse_serving_substations, a declared field, not measured capacity. (access: open; tracks: 1,4; id: peeringdb) https://www.peeringdb.com/
- **PG&E GIS Data Hub**: Selected PG&E infrastructure and risk GIS layers as ArcGIS services. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: pge-gis-hub) https://pgegishub.pge.com/
- **PG&E GRIP user guide and quick start (REST endpoint)**: Documents PG&E load ICA: 576 hourly results per line section for four limiting criteria, feeder CSV/ZIP downloads, no-login ArcGIS REST at services2.arcgis.com/mJaJSax0KPHoCNB6/arcgis/rest/services/DRPComplianceRelProd/FeatureServer. Values are directional, not a guarantee. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: pge-grip-guide) https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/docs/about/doing-business-with-pge/pge-grip-quick-start-guide.pdf
- **PNNL IM3 Open Source Data Center Atlas**: Open geospatial inventory of existing US data center locations compiled by Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: im3-datacenter-atlas) https://im3.pnnl.gov/datacenter-atlas
- **PUDL (Public Utility Data Liberation), Catalyst Cooperative**: Cleaned and linked EIA, FERC and EPA data in SQLite and Parquet. Removes most of the cleanup work of using raw EIA/FERC files. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: pudl) https://github.com/catalyst-cooperative/pudl
- **RIPE Atlas public measurements (BigQuery)**: Ping, traceroute, DNS, HTTP measurements from deployed probes since 2020, queryable in BigQuery (Google account). - **Virginia DEQ, issued air permits for data centers**: List of air permits for data center backup generation: facility, locality, permit ID, dates. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: va-deq-air-permits) https://www.deq.virginia.gov/news-info/shortcuts/permits/air/issued-air-permits-for-data-centers
- **WRI Aqueduct water risk atlas**: Global water stress and water risk indicators by watershed. Relevant to cooling water availability. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: wri-aqueduct) https://www.wri.org/aqueduct

### APIs (6)

- **Census Reporter API** by Census Reporter: Simplified access to ACS tables by geography through api.censusreporter.org. No key, unlike the official Census API. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: census-reporter-api) https://censusreporter.org/
- **Google Solar API**: Per-building roof area, panel layouts, sunshine hours and estimated yearly solar output. Needs a Google Cloud project with billing and an API key. (access: api-key; tracks: 1,3; id: google-solar-api) https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/solar/overview
- **NOAA Climate Data Online API v2**: Station observations and climate normals as JSON. Token header; 5 requests/s, 10,000/day; max 1,000 results per call. (access: api-key; tracks: 1,3; id: noaa-cdo-api) https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/webservices/v2
- **Open-Meteo weather API** by Open-Meteo (Patrick Zippenfenig): Weather forecasts and historical reanalysis by coordinate, hourly, JSON. No key for non-commercial use. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: open-meteo) https://open-meteo.com/
- **Overpass API (OpenStreetMap queries)** by OpenStreetMap community (Roland Olbricht): Query OpenStreetMap features by tag and area, e.g. power=substation, power=line with voltage, building footprints. No key; public endpoints are rate-limited. OSM coverage of grid assets is volunteer-mapped and uneven. (access: open; tracks: 1,2; id: overpass-api) https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API
- **USGS Water Data APIs**: REST/OGC APIs for streamflow, groundwater, daily and continuous values at 1M+ monitoring sites. Not a water right or utility allocation. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: usgs-water-data) https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/

### Simulators and benchmarks (5)

- **EnergyPlus (now under NatLabRockies)** by US DOE, NREL (now NLR): Whole-building energy and HVAC simulation with Python API. Needs facility inputs; not a calibrated data center model out of the box. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: energyplus) https://github.com/NatLabRockies/EnergyPlus
- **pvlib-python** by pvlib community (Sandia origin): Python functions for solar position, irradiance, PV cell temperature and DC/AC output. The standard open PV performance model. BSD-3. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: pvlib-python) https://github.com/pvlib/pvlib-python
- **REopt (NREL, now NLR)** by NREL (now NLR): Optimizes the size and dispatch of on-site solar, storage and generators for a site's load and tariff. Julia package and web API. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: nrel-reopt) https://github.com/NREL/REopt.jl
- **reV (Renewable Energy Potential model, NREL, now NLR)** by NREL (now NLR): Geospatial model of renewable generation potential, supply curves and siting exclusions. (access: open; tracks: 1; id: nrel-rev) https://github.com/NREL/reV
- **System Advisor Model (SAM, NREL, now NLR)** by NREL (now NLR): Performance and financial models for solar, wind, storage and other systems. Python access through PySAM. (access: open; tracks: 1,3; id: nrel-sam) https://sam.nlr.gov/
